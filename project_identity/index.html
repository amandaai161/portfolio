    Identity 2.0

    • Brand Design
    • Web Design
    • Canva Brand Kit

    A full rebrand for Identity, a global community serving more than 1,000 active members. It was never just an aesthetic refresh. The harder part was building a visual system that could scale with the community and still be run by a small team with very limited resources.

    Year
    2026
    Duration
    7 weeks
    Company
    Identity
    Industry
    Community

    Three key design decisions that shaped the outcome.

    • A system a volunteer can run

      The brand ships as a Canva kit, not a sixty-page PDF. Chapter leads across 38 cities produce on-brand material without a designer in the loop, which is the only way a small team keeps up with a community this size.

    • One mark, many chapters

      A single core mark with a defined slot for each chapter, so 38 local groups read as one network instead of 38 separate brands — and a new chapter joins without anyone redrawing anything.

    • Rules where they matter, room where they don’t

      Type, spacing and the logo lockup are fixed. Color and imagery are open. That split is what keeps the system usable at volume without the output going flat.
