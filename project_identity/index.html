    Identity 2.0

    A full rebrand for Identity, a global community serving more than 1,000 active members. It was never just an aesthetic refresh. The harder part was building a visual system that could scale with the community and still be run by a small team with very limited resources.

    Year
    2026
    Duration
    7 weeks
    Company
    Identity
    Industry
    Community

    Key design decisions

    • Built for a core of 30 members, not the whole 1,000+ community at once

      Pushing one message to everyone had stopped working. A small active core could spread it outward.

    • Turned 40+ scattered chapters into one interactive map of the network

      Members find their own city, then open it to see who is there, what they run, and who leads it.

    • Shipped the system in Canva, because the people posting are not designers

      Figma or Illustrator would need a designer every time. Canva holds the quality and any chapter lead can use it.

    • Designed the logo, naming and colours as one family any new chapter can join

      Diversity is what this community runs on, so the system stretches to fit new cities and fields instead of being redrawn.
