Three key design decisions that shaped the outcome.

  • Premium is pace, not ornament

    The page moves slowly and holds still. A scroll-scrubbed film of the bottle earns attention where a carousel of product shots would have spent it, because at this price point the buyer is being asked to slow down.

  • Moroccan origin without the cliché

    Provenance is carried by material, color and typographic restraint rather than pattern or postcard imagery, so the product reads as authentic instead of themed.

  • One product, one path

    There is a single route through the page — origin, benefit, product, buy. A three-SKU brand does not need navigation. It needs a sequence, and every link on the page points along it.
