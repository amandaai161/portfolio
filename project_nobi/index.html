A digital storefront and landing page for NOBI Bakery — where time-honored Japanese baking meets modern flavors, wrapped in a seamless ordering experience for busy city dwellers.

Year
2024
Duration
2 weeks
Company
NOBI Bakery
Industry
F&B

Three key design decisions that shaped the outcome.

  • Ordering is the design

    Everything on the page routes toward one action. Product, price and the way to buy stay within reach at every scroll depth, because the visitor is buying breakfast, not browsing a brand.

  • Japanese by structure, not decoration

    The restraint comes from the grid, the vertical type and the space left around the product — not from applied motifs — so the heritage reads as craft rather than costume.

  • Built for a phone on a commute

    Body type never drops below 16px, tap targets clear 44px, and the hero footage is short and muted. The storefront has to work one-handed on mobile data, because that is where the order actually gets placed.
